## Product name

**BlakeLabs Multimedia**

## Short description

Professional local audio and video conversion with safe presets, advanced controls and private on-device processing.

## Full description

BlakeLabs Multimedia is a focused desktop workspace for converting audio and video without uploading your files.

Choose a professional preset or customize video quality, bitrate, resolution, encoder speed, audio bitrate, sample rate, channels, loudness and metadata. FFmpeg runs in the background while the interface reports progress, speed and ETA.

Built for practical everyday workflows:

- convert MOV, MKV, WebM and other video to broadly compatible MP4;
- create compact MP4 and WebM output;
- extract high-quality MP3 audio;
- create FLAC lossless and 24-bit WAV output;
- generate looping GIFs;
- process multiple files through a managed queue;
- keep source files safe with atomic output publication.

Your selected media stays on your computer. BlakeLabs Multimedia does not require an account and does not upload media files.
